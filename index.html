03 — Process

How We Verify
Your Documents

A rigorous multi-layer ML pipeline that leaves no room for fraud.

📤

Document Submission

Client securely uploads documents via our encrypted portal. Supports PDF, JPG, PNG — ITR forms, PAN cards, bank statements, insurance papers.

Encrypted Upload
🔍

Metadata Extraction

Our system reads hidden metadata — creation timestamps, software signatures, edit history, and pixel-level data to detect any modifications.

Forensic Scan
🤖

ML Model Analysis

Our trained model cross-references 200+ fraud patterns, font inconsistencies, seal authenticity, and layout anomalies against real document databases.

AI Processing
🧾

Cross Verification

Document data is validated against government APIs — Income Tax portal, UIDAI, and banking networks to confirm identity and financial claims.

API Cross-Check
📊

Risk Scoring

Each document receives a fraud risk score from 0–100. Documents above threshold are flagged and routed for manual human review instantly.

Scoring Engine
✅

Final Report

A detailed verification report is delivered to your dashboard — authentic, tampered, or suspicious — with full evidence trail for legal compliance.

Verified Report
